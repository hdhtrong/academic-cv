## About Me

I am an AI researcher and software engineer with a Master's degree in Information Technology and research experience in Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and Data Mining.

My work focuses on developing intelligent systems that learn from heterogeneous data sources, including electronic health records (EHR), omics data, medical imaging, textual information, and user behavior sequences.

I am particularly interested in bridging academic research and real-world AI applications through robust machine learning systems and multimodal foundation models.
